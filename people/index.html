Our team

The people behind the research

The LISN Lab brings together postdoctoral researchers, PhD students across Lancaster, Manchester, and Glasgow, and undergraduate research assistants in S.T.A.R.S., all studying how inner speech connects mind, body, and behaviour.

PI and Research Staff

Bo Yao

Lab Director

Leads research on how inner speech connects language, mind, and body, spanning everyday thinking, voice hearing, reading, and AI.
