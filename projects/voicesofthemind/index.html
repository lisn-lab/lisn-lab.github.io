Exploring Voices of the Mind

Empowering Youth in the Northwest with Art, Science, and Technology

Introduction

‘Voices of the Mind’ is a public engagement project exploring inner speech – the silent words and conversations that run through our minds.

We aim to empower young people aged 14-18 from diverse backgrounds with knowledge about their inner experiences, create interdisciplinary dialogue between science and art, reduce stigma around different thought patterns, and make neuroscience accessible to those traditionally underrepresented in STEM fields.

Our project brings together scientists, artists, and young people to explore how our brains generate inner thoughts, how technology can help us understand these processes, and how creative expression can illuminate our internal experiences.

    VoiceScape: Interactive Web App

    Try VoiceScape →

    Composer Charlie Brown built a physical launchpad installation for our live workshops and launch event. So that anyone can explore it from home, we created this interactive web version.

    How it works

    Use your keyboard or mobile tap grid to layer pre-recorded words and phrases describing young people’s inner voices over Charlie’s ambient soundtrack. You can also record your own phrases, assign them to keys, and export an MP3 of your composition.

    Your recordings and exported audio stay strictly on your own device and are never uploaded to a server. After you play, the app invites brief optional feedback and anonymous background information to help us understand who visits the project.

    Artist Credits: Background music by Charlie Brown. Voice clips recorded by Alice Evans from workshop participants’ transcripts.

    From the Workshops
